## 11. Paragraph structure
Paragraphs are short, often one to three sentences, with many single-line paragraphs used for emphasis or pacing. Dialogue frequently receives its own line, followed by a separate paragraph of reaction or interpretation. This produces a web-novel rhythm: easy to scan, emotionally segmented, and focused on moment-by-moment shifts.

Transitions are usually explicit rather than lyrical: “After that,” “Then,” “Eventually,” “A few days later,” “In the end.” The prose also uses abrupt paragraph breaks for intrusive thoughts or realizations, especially with lines like “This is strange” or “I messed up.” Scene movement tends to be orderly—room, kitchen, elevator, entrance—rather than associative.
