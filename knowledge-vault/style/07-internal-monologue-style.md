## 7. Internal monologue style
**Sendai:** Sendai’s internal monologue is polished, analytical, and full of social calculation. She often asks rhetorical questions—“What’s with her?” or “Just what is going through her head?”—then answers herself with a practical or cynical conclusion. Her thoughts frequently turn into mini-essays about school hierarchy, family expectations, or why a situation is inconvenient.

**Miyagi:** Miyagi’s internal monologue is more repetitive, defensive, and denial-driven. Realizations usually arrive as blunt statements—“This wasn’t jealousy,” “It wasn’t a big deal”—followed by qualifications that reveal the opposite. She often interrupts herself with restrained em-dash thoughts, withheld phrases, and self-corrections, especially when the topic approaches friendship, abandonment, or wanting Sendai nearby.
