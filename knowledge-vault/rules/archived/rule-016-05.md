# Rule

Render 借りっぱなしが嫌 and similar borrowing-obligation phrasing as not wanting to remain indebted or owing someone, rather than literally continuing to borrow something.

---

**Archived round 3.** Added as a third concrete example under [[rule-000-01]] (general idiomatic-rendering rule), where it joins 毒されてる and 機嫌が悪くなる. A separate rule for a single idiom fragments the idiom rule unnecessarily.
