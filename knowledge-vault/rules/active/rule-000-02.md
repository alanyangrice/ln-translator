# Rule

Do not add specifics, intensifiers, or implications that are not present in the Japanese source. Match the source's level of specificity — neither narrower (inventing exact numbers, named feelings, or sexual implications) nor broader (collapsing distinct phrasings into a generic equivalent).

## Examples

- 再生速度を速める → "increased the playback speed", not "double the speed".
- 変態 → "pervert" alone — do not add "who got off on..." or other sexual-gratification implications the source does not state.
