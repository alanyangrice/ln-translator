# Rule

Use 「…」 and 『…』 **only** for what the human reference uses them for, and use English curly quotes ("…") for everything else.

- **Use 「…」** for primary spoken dialogue lines, exactly as the human reference does.
- **Use 『…』** for nested quotations, embedded quotations, and text messages.
- **Use English curly quotes ("…")** for non-dialogue scare quotes, labels, emphasis quotes, and mid-narration quoted snippets (e.g. the narrator referring to a concept by name, or mentioning a short spoken phrase inside a narration sentence rather than presenting it as its own live dialogue line).

Never substitute English curly quotes for a standalone live dialogue line, and never use 「」/『』 for non-dialogue emphasis.
Never use Markdown emphasis (`*...*` or `_..._`) in the translation itself; this is prose, not formatted Markdown.

## Why

Two complementary failure modes:

1. **Reaching for English convention inside standalone dialogue.** When a spoken line is presented as its own dialogue line, the model often switches to "…" even though the chapter uses 「…」 for speech.
2. **Over-applying Japanese brackets to non-speech.** The model also over-corrects — wrapping scare quotes, concept labels, or emphatic phrases ("the *little bet*") in 「」 even though those aren't dialogue. The human reference uses English curly quotes for those.

Keep brackets consistent with what's actually being quoted, not with the surrounding language convention.

## Examples

- **Standalone dialogue (use 「」):** `「今日呼んでないんだけど」` → `「I didn't ask you over today.」`
- **Nested / messages (use 『』):** A text message rendered inside narration → `『家に来てる』` → `『I'm at your place.』`
- **Mid-narration quoted spoken snippet (use "…"):** `「はい」と答えると、「了解！」と返ってくる` → `when I answered, "Yes," she replied with an energetic, "Got it!"`
- **Scare quote / concept label (use "…"):** `“小さな賭け”` → `"little bet"`, **not** `「little bet」`.
- **Almost-spoken internal line (use "…", not italics):** `これからする質問に全部答えて、と言いかけて飲み込む` → `"Answer every question I'm about to ask you," I almost said, but swallowed the words.`, **not** `*Answer every question...*`.
