# Rule

Preserve Japanese-style brackets for all quoted speech: 「…」 for primary spoken dialogue and 『…』 for nested or embedded quotations and messages, exactly as the human reference does. Never substitute English curly quotes ("…") even when the quotation appears mid-narration as a remembered or paraphrased line.

## Why

Mid-narrative quotations ("the LLM said "X" earlier") are a common slip point: the model reaches for English convention even when the surrounding narration uses 「」. Keep the brackets consistent throughout the chapter.
