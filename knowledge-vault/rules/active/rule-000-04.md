# Rule

Preserve the grammatical subject and agency from the source. If the narrator describes their own feelings or actions being exposed, do not rewrite the sentence so that another character is the active subject — keep the ownership of the action where the Japanese has it.

## Example

- 感情を露わにした私 ≈ "my emotions were exposed" / "I let my emotions show" — not "she dragged my emotions out", which flips the subject from the narrator to the other character.
