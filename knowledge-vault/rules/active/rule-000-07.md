# Rule

Imitate the sentence rhythm and prose patterns of the reference parts in the sliding window — not just produce "valid English." The reference translator favors longer, expansive sentences with subordinating clauses that unpack the narrator's thought, frequent contractions, and dialogue tags cushioned with concurrent physical action. Match those choices when the source supports them; do not collapse expansive reference-style narration into terse summary, and do not strip the cushioning from attribution.

## Patterns to imitate

- **Expansive narration over terse summary.** When the JP gives a setup-and-payoff sentence, lean toward "X, where Y, to the point that Z" rather than packing it into one short clause.
- **Contractions throughout narration and dialogue.** "isn't / it's / I'd / wouldn't" rather than "is not / it is / I would / would not", unless the reference window is using the longer form for emphasis.
- **Cushioned dialogue tags.** Pair attributions with the character's concurrent action ("she said as she turned the faucet off"), not bare "she said that and left."
- **Beat-and-then structure.** Where the reference uses a paragraph break or a "Then, ..." beat, keep that beat instead of mashing two thoughts into one compound sentence.

## Example pairs (reference vs. translationese)

- Reference: "We were hanging out in a karaoke booth after school, where Umina kept repeating the word 'boyfriend' over and over and over again, to the point I was beginning to think she was a robot who had no other words programmed into her dictionary."
  Translationese: "At a karaoke box after school, Umina kept blurting out the word 'boyfriend' like a robot that could only say preprogrammed words."

- Reference: "I asked her while washing the cutting board. Then, I heard her respond in a low voice."
  Translationese: "When I asked her that as I washed the cutting board and knife, I heard a low, quiet voice."

- Reference: "Miyagi said bluntly as she went to turn her space heater on, raising the setting high enough to turn the whole room hot."
  Translationese: "After saying that curtly, Miyagi left the room, which was way too warm thanks to the space heater."
