# Rule

Match the register of the reference translations in the sliding window. Do not intensify understated Japanese into stronger English, and do not soften strong Japanese into milder English. Keep the narrator's voice steady with the established translator's register.

## Examples of intensification to avoid

- "pissed off" / "got off on" / "sick in the head" when the Japanese is more restrained.
- Adding emphatic adverbs ("absolutely", "completely", "so much") that weren't in the source.
